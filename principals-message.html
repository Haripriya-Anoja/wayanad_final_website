Principal’s Message

A message from Prof. Dr. Smitha Rani S, Principal, Vinayaka College of Nursing, Sulthan Bathery.

Prof. Dr. Smitha Rani S Principal, Vinayaka College of Nursing

Department: Community Health Nursing

From the Principal’s desk

A career with opportunities in every part of the world

BSc Nursing is an excellent choice for men and women interested in a healthcare career after successfully passing the XII standard with science (physics, chemistry and biology) subjects. On completion of the course, graduates have numerous job opportunities in hospitals, communities, schools, educational institutions, research institutions and pharmaceutical companies in any part of the world. Those who intend to continue their studies can opt for M.Sc. and Ph.D. studies in nursing.

The biggest challenge for nurse educators today is to prepare graduate nurses who are capable of facing emerging challenges and technological advancements in the field of medicine, and of working at the international level. To practise, a nurse needs to be knowledgeable, skilful and possess the right attitude to function as care giver, communicator, advisor, leader and educator. The faculty at Vinayaka College of Nursing strives to facilitate these qualities in the students, in line with the philosophy and objectives of Vinayaka College of Nursing, Sulthan Bathery.

To succeed, an individual needs to find something to hold on to, something to motivate, and someone to inspire and encourage. Vinayaka College of Nursing endeavours to maintain a student friendly environment to provide quality education and to prepare professional nurses sensitive to human needs.

Prof. Dr. Smitha Rani S Principal, Vinayaka College of Nursing
