Admission

Merit seats are allotted through the LBS Centre for Science and Technology; management and NRI seats are handled separately. The documents required at the time of joining are listed below.

How to apply

Admission process

  • Merit seats

    Merit seats are allotted through the LBS Centre for Science and Technology (www.lbscentre.kerala.gov.in).

  • Management and NRI seats

    Management and NRI seats are handled separately through the college (help@pncmak.in).

  • Submit the documents

    Submit the original documents listed on this page at the time of joining.

  • Pay the college fee

    Fees are remitted to the trust account given below. Bring the payment acknowledgement when you report.

Important
The old website does not publish admission dates, application deadlines, seat numbers or fee amounts. Confirm current dates and fees with the college office before publishing this page.

Checklist

Original documents to be submitted at the time of joining

  1. Allotment Memo
  2. SSLC Marklist
  3. +2 Marklist
  4. Conduct Certificate
  5. Medical Fitness Certificate
  6. Income Certificate
  7. Transfer Certificate
  8. Passport size photograph with white background (colour) – 10 copies
  9. Passport size photograph in CD, to the specification 150 × 200, below 30 KB, JPEG
  10. Copy of AADHAAR card
Eligibility at a glance
BSc Nursing: +2 or equivalent with 50% marks in Physics, Chemistry, Biology and English, as per INC norms; should have completed 17 years of age as on 31st December; no upper age limit.
GNM: +2 or equivalent in any group.
