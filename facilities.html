Teaching and learning

How clinical skills are built

The college follows a cycle of demonstration, practice and supervised clinical work.

  • Well-equipped laboratories

    Laboratories with state-of-the-art, modern equipment support skill practice before students enter the clinical area.

  • Teaching by demonstration

    Teaching is followed by demonstration and re-demonstration by the students.

  • Clinical and community exposure

    Proper and routine clinical exposure, opportunities for observational visits, and both urban and rural community nursing services.

Remedial coaching
Remedial coaching classes are arranged for under-achieved students to ensure their success.
