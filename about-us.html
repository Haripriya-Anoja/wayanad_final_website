About Us

Vinayaka College & School of Nursing, Sulthan Bathery, Wayanad — established by the Vinayaka Educational & Charitable Trust in 2011.

Who we are

Planting and harvesting well qualified, skilful nurses

Vinayaka College & School of Nursing has its goal of planting and harvesting well qualified, skilful and eminent nurses to meet the health needs of the population irrespective of caste, creed, race, sex and religion.

To enable further professional growth, and because there is a felt need to develop a deep pride in the nursing profession among the students, Vinayaka Educational & Charitable Trust was formed in 2011 and started the BSc Nursing programme with permission from the Government of Kerala and the Kerala University of Health Sciences, and with approval from the Indian Nursing Council and the Kerala Nurses & Midwives Council.

  • BSc Nursing — 4 years, approved by INC and KNC, affiliated to KUHS.
  • GNM (General Nursing) — 3 years, approved by the Government of Kerala, INC and KNC.
  • Campus of five acres at Sulthan Bathery, in the heart of Wayanad.
  • Routine clinical exposure, observational visits and community nursing services.
