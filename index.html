About the institution

Nursing education in the heart of Wayanad

To enable further professional growth, and because there is a felt need to develop a deep pride in the nursing profession among students, Vinayaka Educational & Charitable Trust was formed in 2011 and started the BSc Nursing programme with permission from the Government of Kerala and Kerala University of Health Sciences, and approval from the Indian Nursing Council and the Kerala Nurses & Midwives Council.

The college and school function from a five acre campus at Sulthan Bathery, with well-equipped laboratories, a library with internet and Wi-Fi facilities, hostel accommodation and routine clinical exposure for every student.

From the Principal’s desk

A student friendly environment for quality nursing education

The biggest challenge for nurse educators today is to prepare graduate nurses who are capable of facing emerging challenges and technological advancements in the field of medicine and of working at the international level. To practise, a nurse needs to be knowledgeable, skilful and possess the right attitude to function as care giver, communicator, advisor, leader and educator.

The faculty at Vinayaka College of Nursing strives to facilitate these qualities in the students in line with the philosophy and objectives of the college.

Prof. Dr. Smitha Rani S Principal, Vinayaka College of Nursing
