Undergraduate degree · 4 years

BSc. Nursing

The Nursing profession — God’s special helper in healing. Vinayaka College & School of Nursing has its goal of planting and harvesting well qualified, skilful and eminent nurses to meet the health needs of the population irrespective of caste, creed, race, sex and religion.

  • Duration4 years
  • ApprovalIndian Nursing Council (INC) and Kerala Nursing Council (KNC)
  • AffiliationKerala University of Health Sciences (KUHS)
  • EligibilityCandidates who have passed +2 or equivalent with 50% marks in Physics, Chemistry, Biology (PCB) and English, as per INC norms.
  • AgeThe candidate should have completed 17 years of age as on 31st December. There is no upper age limit.
